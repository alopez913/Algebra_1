✦ Success Criteria
  • Find cube roots of numbers.
  • Evaluate expressions that contain cube roots.
  • Use cube roots to solve real-life problems.
Vocabulary
cube root perfect cube

A cube root of a number is a number that, when multiplied by itself twice, equals the given number. A perfect cube is a number that can be written as the cube of an integer. The symbol \(\sqrt[3]{\phantom{x}}\) represents a cube root.
